One-Time Firebase Setup Required

To share coaching logs across all devices and logins, connect to Firebase (free). Follow these steps once — takes about 5 minutes:

Step 1 — Create Firebase ProjectGo to console.firebase.google.com → click "Add project" → name it ClusterPhoenix → click through and create.
Step 2 — Enable FirestoreIn your project → click "Firestore Database" in the left menu → "Create database" → choose "Start in test mode" → pick any location → click "Enable".
Step 3 — Get Your Config KeysClick the ⚙️ gear (Project Settings) → scroll to "Your apps" → click "</> Web" → name it anything → copy the firebaseConfig object shown.
Step 4 — Paste Into This FileOpen index.html in a text editor → find the FIREBASE_CONFIG block near the top of the <script> → replace each REPLACE_ME_... value with your actual keys → save → re-upload to Vercel.

After setup, all coaching logs entered by any TL will instantly appear for all other users on any device.

📖 Attendance Policy — Penalty & Points Guide
Attendance points remain on record for 4 months from the date of occurrence. After 4 months, the points are automatically cleared from your active total. Accumulating points within a 4-month window may result in progressive disciplinary action.

📖 Rainmaker Reference Guide

⚡ The Rainmaker Loop

Ask required question → Acknowledge the answer → Find the meaning → Connect a relevant benefit → Check acceptance → Advance the booking
1
Open and Gain Permission
▶
"Hi [Name], this is [Agent] from Homeaglow on a recorded line. I'm reaching out because you expressed interest in getting help with your home — I'd love to learn a bit more about what you need. Do you have a few minutes so I can understand your situation and check the best available option for you?"
2
Required Home Discovery
▶
"Let me ask a few quick questions so I can recommend the right amount of time and the right scope for your first cleaning. How many bedrooms and bathrooms would you like included? Which areas need the most attention on the first visit? Do you have any pets or anyone with allergies the cleaner should know about?"
3
Deepen Pain and Motivation
▶
"You mentioned [answer]. What has made that hardest to keep up with? And when the home is clean and taken care of — what does that feel like for you or your family?"
4
Find the True Barrier
▶
"When you first looked at the offer, what kept you from completing it — was it the price, the membership, the commitment, the timing, or wanting more confidence in the cleaner?"
5
Recommend and Personalize
▶
"Based on [home size / scope / situation], I recommend [hours]. Here's why that works for your home: [personalized reason]. This gives you [specific outcome] and sets you up for a consistent result going forward."
6
Resolve and Verify
▶
"That makes sense. Here is exactly how [concern] works… Does that resolve the concern, or is there another part of it I should address?"
7
Explain Price and Terms
▶
"Today's charge is [amount]. After the first cleaning, your ongoing rate is [rate] per visit on a [frequency] schedule. Can you tell me how you understand today's charge and the future cost?"
8
Close Into Scheduling
▶
"Great — let's secure the cleaning. What day next week works best for you? Morning or afternoon?"
9
Confirm and Reinforce
▶
"Let's review everything once: your appointment is [date/time], the scope is [rooms], today's charge is [amount], and your ongoing rate is [rate]. Is everything correct and do you agree to those terms?"
🔓 Opening & Permission
Required
"Do you have a few minutes so I can understand what you need and check the best available option?"
Follow-up: "I appreciate that — this will only take a few minutes and I want to make sure I match you with the right fit."
Purpose: Establish consent and frame the call as collaborative, not a pitch.
🔍 Reason for Inquiry
Required
"What made you start looking for help with the cleaning?"
Follow-up: "How long has that been a challenge?" / "What's changed recently that made this a priority?"
Purpose: Uncover the emotional and practical motivation driving the inquiry.
Recommended
"Are you currently using any cleaning service or handling it yourself?"
Follow-up: "What's working about that? What isn't?"
Purpose: Identify current baseline and gaps to position Homeaglow's value.
Recommended
"Have you had a professional cleaner before? How did that go?"
Follow-up: "What worked well? What would you want to be different?"
Purpose: Surface prior experience to tailor reassurances and expectations.
🏠 Home Discovery
Required
"How many bedrooms and bathrooms would you like included in the first visit?"
Follow-up: "Any other spaces — kitchen, living room, home office?"
Purpose: Establish scope to build an accurate recommendation.
Required
"Which areas need the most attention on the first cleaning?"
Follow-up: "Is this more of a deep clean situation or more of a maintenance clean?"
Purpose: Prioritize scope and set the right expectation for the first visit.
Recommended
"How would you describe the current condition of the home?"
Follow-up: "Any areas that haven't been cleaned in a while or need extra time?"
Purpose: Adjust hours recommendation and set realistic expectations.
Recommended
"What would a perfect clean look like for you — what's the outcome you're hoping for?"
Follow-up: "If you walked into the home after the cleaning, what would tell you it was done right?"
Purpose: Connect the service to their personal definition of success.
📅 Timing & Frequency
Required
"When would you ideally like the first cleaning completed?"
Follow-up: "Is there a specific event or reason for that timeline?"
Purpose: Create urgency and align scheduling to a real need.
Required
"If the first cleaning goes well, how often would help be useful — weekly, every two weeks, monthly, or only as needed?"
Follow-up: "A lot of customers in similar situations find [frequency] works best — does that sound right for your lifestyle?"
Purpose: Anchor ongoing frequency and move toward a membership conversation.
🐾 Cleaner Fit
Required
"Do you have any pets the cleaner should know about?"
Follow-up: "Are they friendly with strangers? Any areas to keep them out of?"
Purpose: Flag safety and logistics; shows professionalism.
Required
"Does anyone in the home have allergies or require specific cleaning products?"
Follow-up: "We can note that in the service instructions for the cleaner."
Purpose: Demonstrates care and helps avoid issues on the first visit.
Recommended
"Do you have any preference for the cleaner — experience level, language, gender?"
Follow-up: "We'll do our best to match you based on availability and ratings."
Purpose: Addresses trust concerns proactively and personalizes the match.
🚧 Barrier Discovery
Required
"When you first reviewed the offer, what kept you from completing the booking?"
Follow-up: "Was it more about [price / commitment / trust / timing]?"
Purpose: Identify the real objection early so it can be addressed before closing.
Recommended
"Is there anything else holding you back from moving forward today?"
Follow-up: "I want to make sure I address everything before we wrap up."
Purpose: Surface hidden objections before entering the close.
Recommended
"Is budget a factor in what you're looking for today?"
Follow-up: "Just so I can make the right recommendation — what range were you thinking?"
Purpose: Qualify budget sensitivity before presenting price.
💳 Close & Confirmation
Recommended
"Based on everything I've heard, here's what I'd recommend — does this sound right for your situation?"
Follow-up: "Let me tell you why I landed on that."
Purpose: Create buy-in on the recommendation before presenting price.
Recommended
"Given everything we've talked about, do you feel like this is worth trying?"
Follow-up: "What would make you feel more confident about it?"
Purpose: Check perceived value before transitioning to price.
Required
"Before we process anything, can you tell me how you understand today's charge and the future cost?"
Follow-up: Correct any misunderstanding before proceeding.
Purpose: Compliance and clarity — ensures the customer understands what they're agreeing to.
Required
"Great — let's secure the cleaning. What day works best for you?"
Follow-up: "Morning or afternoon? We'll get that on the schedule."
Purpose: The close — move directly to scheduling to lock in commitment.
Required
"Let's review the appointment, scope, charges, and commitment once more. Is everything correct and do you agree?"
Follow-up: State each item clearly and pause for verbal confirmation.
Purpose: Final confirmation — verbal agreement on all key terms before processing.

Click any objection to see the full 6-part response framework.

1
"I need to think about it."
▶
Acknowledge
"That makes complete sense — this is a real commitment and you want to feel good about it."
Clarify
"What part do you want to think through — the price, the cleaner match, the commitment, or something else?"
Suggested Response
"Let's work through that together right now — I have all the information you'd need to make this decision."
Acceptance Check
"Does that address what you were unsure about, or is there still something we should talk through?"
Transition
"If we can resolve that, is there anything else standing in the way of getting started today?"
⚠️ Avoid
Saying "No problem, take your time" and ending the call. Thinking about it without a specific concern is almost always a softer objection in disguise.
2
"Membership" (fee, frequency, or enrollment?)
▶
Acknowledge
"I hear you — the membership structure is a new concept for a lot of people."
Clarify
"Is it the ongoing fee, the cleaning frequency, or the enrollment itself that's the concern?"
Suggested Response
"The membership is what keeps your cleaner consistent and your price protected. Without it, you'd pay a higher rate each time with no continuity."
Acceptance Check
"Does knowing how the membership works change how you feel about it?"
Transition
"If the membership made sense, what else would we need to work through?"
⚠️ Avoid
Lumping all membership concerns together. Always clarify which part — fee, cadence, or concept — before responding.
3
"Commitment" (exact term + cancellation)
▶
Acknowledge
"That's a fair concern — nobody wants to feel locked in."
Clarify
"When you say commitment, are you concerned about the length of time or the cancellation policy?"
Suggested Response
"The minimum term is [X months]. After that, you can cancel anytime. During the term, you can reschedule or skip — you're not tied to a rigid schedule."
Acceptance Check
"Knowing the exact terms, does the commitment feel manageable?"
Transition
"Most customers tell me after the first cleaning they never want to cancel — let's get you to that first experience."
⚠️ Avoid
Using vague language like "it's pretty flexible." State the exact term and cancellation policy clearly — ambiguity creates chargebacks.
4
"Price / too expensive" (which part?)
▶
Acknowledge
"I appreciate your honesty — price is always worth discussing."
Clarify
"Is it the initial charge today, the ongoing rate, or the total cost over time that feels like too much?"
Suggested Response
"Let's look at it this way: [X hours] for [price] works out to [$/hr], which is less than most people spend eating out twice a month — and you get your time back every [frequency]."
Acceptance Check
"When you look at it broken down that way, does the value feel more aligned with what you're getting?"
Transition
"Getting started today locks in your rate and gets your home cleaned sooner — let's get you set up."
⚠️ Avoid
Immediately offering a discount or alternative — first anchor the value. Only explore options after value is fully presented.
5
"One-time cleaning only" (transparent comparison)
▶
Acknowledge
"Totally understand — you just want to try it without committing to anything long-term."
Clarify
"Is that because you're not sure about the quality, the price ongoing, or just the commitment itself?"
Suggested Response
"The one-time rate is [X], while the membership intro rate is [Y] — so you actually get a better first experience AND a lower price by going with the membership. The only difference is a [Z-month] commitment."
Acceptance Check
"Does it make sense to go with the better rate given the difference in price?"
Transition
"Think of the membership as a trial with a commitment — but you still get to cancel after [X months] if it's not the right fit."
⚠️ Avoid
Hiding or minimizing the difference between one-time and membership. Transparency builds trust and reduces cancellations.
6
"Trust / stranger in my home"
▶
Acknowledge
"That's a completely legitimate concern — your home is your safe space and you want to know who's coming in."
Clarify
"Is it more about vetting and background checks, or about not having met the cleaner in person first?"
Suggested Response
"Every cleaner goes through a background check and identity verification. You'll see their profile, ratings, and reviews before they arrive. You can also leave access instructions and add service notes."
Acceptance Check
"Knowing you can see their background and ratings first, does that help with the concern?"
Transition
"Most customers feel much more comfortable once they've seen the profile — let's get you to that first experience."
⚠️ Avoid
Dismissing the concern as irrational. This is one of the most emotionally charged objections — validate fully before presenting facts.
7
"Quality / bad prior cleaner"
▶
Acknowledge
"I'm sorry that happened — a bad experience makes it hard to trust again."
Clarify
"What specifically went wrong — was it the quality of the cleaning itself, communication, or something else?"
Suggested Response
"We let you leave detailed service notes so the cleaner knows exactly what matters to you. You also rate after every visit and can request a different cleaner if needed. If there's an issue, we make it right."
Acceptance Check
"Does knowing you have that level of control and a quality guarantee change how you feel about trying again?"
Transition
"You deserve a clean home — and you deserve a service that actually delivers it. Let's give it one real shot."
⚠️ Avoid
Comparing Homeaglow to the prior bad experience by name or disparaging other services. Focus on what makes this different.
8
"Not ready / timing"
▶
Acknowledge
"Timing is everything — I don't want to push you into something that doesn't fit your life right now."
Clarify
"What's happening in your life that makes now feel too soon? Is there an event or milestone you're waiting on?"
Suggested Response
"We can schedule the first visit 2–3 weeks out — you don't have to start this week. Locking in the rate today means you get the offer as it stands, and we build around your schedule."
Acceptance Check
"If we set the first appointment around [their event / future date], does that feel more workable?"
Transition
"Let's lock in the date that works for you — you stay in control of when it happens."
⚠️ Avoid
Pressing urgency in a way that feels dishonest. If timing is a real concern, align to their actual timeline — a booked appointment at a future date is still a win.
9
"Need to ask someone" (spouse / partner)
▶
Acknowledge
"Of course — major decisions are better made together."
Clarify
"What do you think their main concern will be? Price, the commitment, or something else?"
Suggested Response
"Let's resolve your concerns first — if you're confident in it, that conversation becomes a lot easier. What would make you personally feel good about moving forward?"
Acceptance Check
"If [partner] was on the call right now and asked about [likely concern], what would you want me to tell them?"
Transition
"Once you're satisfied, would you feel comfortable making the decision — or would it be better to loop them in now?"
⚠️ Avoid
Asking for a callback without resolving the present customer's own concerns first. Surface and address their objection before involving a third party.
10
"Found another cleaner"
▶
Acknowledge
"That's great — it sounds like you're serious about getting help. Good for you."
Clarify
"What stood out about them that made them feel like a better fit — was it price, schedule, no commitment, or something else?"
Suggested Response
"I want to make sure you're comparing accurately. [Address the specific factor they named — rate, vetting, consistency.] With us, you also get [relevant differentiator]."
Acceptance Check
"Knowing that, does Homeaglow still feel like it could be the right fit — or is the other option clearly better on [specific factor]?"
Transition
"If the comparison is close, the intro offer gives you a low-risk way to find out. You can always revisit after the first cleaning."
⚠️ Avoid
Badmouthing the competition. Ask what made them better and compare accurately — emotional validation plus honest comparison is more persuasive than pressure.
