One-Time Firebase Setup Required

To share coaching logs across all devices and logins, connect to Firebase (free). Follow these steps once — takes about 5 minutes:

Step 1 — Create Firebase ProjectGo to console.firebase.google.com → click "Add project" → name it ClusterPhoenix → click through and create.
Step 2 — Enable FirestoreIn your project → click "Firestore Database" in the left menu → "Create database" → choose "Start in test mode" → pick any location → click "Enable".
Step 3 — Get Your Config KeysClick the ⚙️ gear (Project Settings) → scroll to "Your apps" → click "</> Web" → name it anything → copy the firebaseConfig object shown.
Step 4 — Paste Into This FileOpen index.html in a text editor → find the FIREBASE_CONFIG block near the top of the <script> → replace each REPLACE_ME_... value with your actual keys → save → re-upload to Vercel.

After setup, all coaching logs entered by any TL will instantly appear for all other users on any device.

📖 Attendance Policy — Penalty & Points Guide
Attendance points remain on record for 4 months from the date of occurrence. After 4 months, the points are automatically cleared from your active total. Accumulating points within a 4-month window may result in progressive disciplinary action.
Occurrence Types
TypeDefinition
LateStarted shift after the scheduled start time. Points based on combined late + early-out minutes.
AbandonedLeft shift before scheduled end time without prior approval. Points based on combined late + early-out minutes.
AbsentFull-day absence — no Hubstaff login recorded for a scheduled workday.
NCNSNo-Call No-Show — absent with no prior notice. Manually recorded by management as a critical infraction.
Point Calculation — Based on Combined Late + Early-Out Time
Points are calculated from the total combined minutes of late arrival + early departure in a single shift. If an agent is both late and leaves early, both durations are added together before determining the point value.
