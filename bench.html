How a part is scored. Not word for word. A stage is done if it happened and achieved its purpose however it was worded, partial if it got part of the way, missed only if its purpose was not served. Partial carries half weight. A stage the call never reached is left out of the total rather than scored zero.

What counts as a QA finding. Only something the agent said that was wrong — a wrong offer, price, hours or terms, a misrepresentation, an overpromise, a disclosure withheld before agreement, a DNC request ignored. Sequencing and thin discovery are call-flow issues and report there.
